# ***ScrollFlow for YouTube Extension***



<i>A lightweight Chrome extension that explores improved scrolling behaviour and a sticky video player experience on the YouTube watch page.</i>



* ##### <i>**Features**</i>



<i>\* Sticky video player while scrolling</i>

<i>\* Cleaner and more focused viewing layout</i>

<i>\* Lightweight and distraction-free behaviour</i>



* ##### <i>**Vision**</i>



<i>This project experiments with smarter scrolling interactions to make multitasking on the YouTube watch page more comfortable—especially while browsing recommendations and comments during video playback.</i>



* ##### <i>**Current Limitation**</i>



<i>Because YouTube dynamically controls layout and scrolling through internal scripts, one minor visual issue may appear in certain cases:</i>

<i>while performing a full left-side page scroll, the right recommendation column can momentarily appear visually cropped.</i>



<i>This does \*\*not affect functionality\*\*, but highlights an interesting UX constraint imposed by the platform’s internal rendering model.</i>

<i>Solving this natively at the platform level could unlock a smoother multitasking experience for millions of users.</i>



* ##### <i>**Tech Stack**</i>



<i>\* JavaScript</i>

<i>\* CSS</i>

<i>\* Chrome Extension (Manifest v3)</i>



* ##### <i>**Future Possibilities**</i>
